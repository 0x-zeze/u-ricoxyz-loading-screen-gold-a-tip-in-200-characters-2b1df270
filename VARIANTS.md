# Loading Screen Gold — 5 varian tip (satu baris, maks 200 karakter)

Audiens: developer stres + AI coding agent di tengah build.
Struktur: 2–3 ketukan eskalasi dengan alat/perintah konkret, punchline =
category error / pembalikan. Klise yang dihindari: "it's a feature",
"turn it off and on again", "99% loading" polos, "coffee" generik.

## V1
Tip: while this loads, git blame the flaky test, rebase your weekend, and remember the rollback already filed a bug against you.

## V2 (PEMENANG — direvisi: punchline dipadatkan menjadi "the legacy code is you")
Loading: CI passed, the flaky test failed, and the deploy blamed you — congratulations, the legacy code is you.

## V3
Tip: your merge conflict resolved itself by deleting Friday, and npm install just billed your weekend to the build.

## V4
The agent fixed your bug, broke the build, and opened a ticket against you — your manager already assigned it to you.

## V5
Stuck at 99%? Relax: the build is not frozen, it is just waiting for you to confess the flaky test was your code.

## Alasan pilihan V2
- Tiga ketukan eskalasi konkret dan berurutan (CI passed → flaky test failed → deploy blamed you), masing-masing satu kata kerja sistem yang dikenali audiens.
- Punchline category error yang tajam: developer yang stres justru berubah menjadi "legacy code" — sistem memperlakukan manusia sebagai artefak yang harus diganti, bukan sebaliknya.
- Paling ekonomis: tiap kata earning its place, tanpa setup berlebih; "congratulations" membalik nada kemenangan menjadi vonis.
- Fresh: tidak memakai klise apa pun; "legacy code" adalah ketakutan sehari-hari developer yang dikenali langsung.
- V4 kuat tapi punchline-nya prosedural (tiket + manager), bukan pembalikan kategori; V1/V3/V5 punchline-nya lebih lemah atau setup-nya lebih panjang.
